REC-006 — Business Strategy Case Study

B2B Business Model Optimization

Running a coworking space meant living with its numbers, not just reporting on them. When profitability was inconsistent across workspace types and seasons, I used the business's own operational and financial data to decide what to change.

Role
Founder & General Manager
Context
Techno Club Workspace, 2023–2026
Method
Operational & financial analysis
Outcome
+40% revenue · −20% opex
Problem

Inconsistent profitability across workspace types and seasons.

Analysis of operational and financial data surfaced profitability differences across workspace types, seasonality risk, capacity constraints, and the specific cost drivers behind them — the picture wasn't uniform, and the pricing model wasn't accounting for it.

Approach

A strategic pivot toward B2B, backed by the data.

Based on that analysis, I executed a strategic shift toward a B2B model targeting startups, work teams, and corporate instructors, moving to upfront monthly contracts and improving space utilization rather than relying on unpredictable walk-in demand.

Result

A 40% revenue increase and a 20% cut in operating expenses.

The business model optimization, combined with related operational improvements and technology-driven process changes, contributed to a 40% increase in revenue and a 20% reduction in operational expenses.
