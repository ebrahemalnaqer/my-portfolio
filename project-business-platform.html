REC-001 — Technology Case Study

Business Management & Operations Platform

Running a coworking business meant juggling clients, sessions, payments, inventory and shift handovers across disconnected spreadsheets and manual notes. I built a single web-based platform to hold all of it.

Role
Founder & Platform Developer
Type
Web-based business platform
Stack
Google Apps Script · JavaScript · Google Sheets
Date
Sep 2026
Problem

Fragmented, manual operational processes.

CRM, attendance, point-of-sale, invoicing, inventory, financial records, shift reconciliation and customer feedback were each tracked separately — slow to update, easy to lose track of, and hard to report on as a whole.

Approach

A centralized platform with a modular backend.

I designed and developed a web-based business management platform integrating CRM, attendance, POS, invoicing, inventory, financial records, shift reconciliation, feedback, and KPI dashboards, built on a modular Google Apps Script backend for clients, sessions, services, invoices, expenses, cash shifts, and feedback.

Result

Automated operations end to end, with visibility built in.

Check-in/out, session charging, package balance tracking, invoice generation, inventory updates, and financial aggregation are now automated rather than manual, and the KPI dashboard gives a single place to see how the business is actually performing.
