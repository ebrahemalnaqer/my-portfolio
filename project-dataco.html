REC-003 — Best Graduation Project, NTI Data Analysis Track

DataCo Smart Supply Chain for Big Data Analysis

A 5-member capstone team was handed a 180,000-row, 53-variable retail supply chain dataset and one question: sales are $36.78M, so why does profit look 50% smaller than it should? I led the sales & profitability analysis workstream.

Role
Lead Sales & Profitability Analyst
Team
5 members
Dataset
180K+ records · 53 variables
Tools
SQL · Power BI · Star Schema
Problem

$36.78M in sales, but the profit line didn't match.

The dataset covered a global retail supply chain, but leadership had no reliable way to see where the 50% profit gap was actually coming from — discount policy turned out to be the biggest driver.

Approach

Model it, then interrogate it with SQL.

I engineered a star-schema data model to make 180K+ records and 53 variables queryable at speed, then used advanced SQL with CTEs, window functions, and multi-table joins to trace exactly which transactions were eroding margin.

Result

A capped discount policy contributing to $2.2M in net profit savings.

The analysis traced $3.73M in potential lost revenue to inefficient discounting, and identified a 10–13% optimal discount range where volume held up. I proposed a 5% discount cap, which modeled out to $2.2M in net profit savings.
