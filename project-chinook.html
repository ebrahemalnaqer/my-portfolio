REC-004 — Strategic BI Case Study

Chinook Strategic Business Intelligence Project

A digital music storefront had a fixed base of 59 customers, with more than a third of revenue riding on a single genre. The brief was to turn raw sales data into an actionable growth roadmap.

Role
BI Analyst
Customer base
59 global customers
Genre dependency
37% on Rock
Tools
Power BI · Geospatial Analysis
Problem

Revenue concentration, hiding behind a steady-looking storefront.

Customer, sales, and product data showed a static 59-customer base and a 37% revenue dependency on Rock alone — a level of concentration that left the business exposed to a single genre's performance.

Approach

A commission-based sales strategy, guided by a market map.

I analyzed customer, sales, and product data to identify revenue concentration and growth opportunities, developed a commission-based sales strategy based on the patterns found, and used geospatial analysis in Power BI to identify high-performing international markets.

Result

An executive dashboard built to track the strategy going forward.

The output was an executive Power BI dashboard mapping high-performing international markets and sales performance — giving the business a live view of where growth was actually coming from, not just a one-time report.
