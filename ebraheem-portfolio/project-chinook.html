REC-002 — Strategic BI Case Study

Chinook Strategic Business Intelligence Project

A digital music storefront had flatlined: a fixed base of 59 customers and more than a third of revenue riding on a single genre. The brief was to turn raw sales requirements into an actionable growth roadmap.

Role
BI Analyst
Customer base
59 (static)
Genre dependency
37% on Rock
Tools
Power BI · Geospatial Analysis
Problem

Growth stagnation, hiding behind a healthy-looking storefront.

On the surface the store was operating normally. Underneath, it had a static 59-customer base, a 37% revenue dependency on Rock alone, a catalog full of historical "zero-sales" assets never activated, and sales structurally capped by a rigid 7-invoice ceiling per customer relationship.

Approach

Turn the diagnosis into a commission-based structure and a market map.

I formulated a targeted, commission-based sales structure designed specifically to activate the catalog's zero-sales assets and route around the invoice ceiling rather than fight it directly. In parallel, I used geospatial data inside Power BI to map which international markets were actually performing, so marketing budget could follow demand instead of habit.

Result

An executive dashboard built to keep tracking the fix.

The output was an executive-level Power BI dashboard, built to visualization best practices, tracking user acquisition and sales performance on an ongoing basis — giving the business a live view of whether the new sales structure and market reallocation were actually working, not just a one-time report.
