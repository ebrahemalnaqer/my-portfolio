REC-007 — Automation Case Study

Booking Automation Workflow

Manual booking meant double-bookings, missed conflicts, and time spent reconciling calendars by hand. I built an automated workflow to validate and manage reservations instead.

Role
Founder & General Manager
Context
Techno Club Workspace
Stack
Google Apps Script · Google Sheets · Google Calendar
Outcome
~60% fewer booking conflicts
Problem

A manual booking workflow, prone to conflicts.

Bookings tracked by hand across spreadsheets and calendars left room for double-bookings and scheduling conflicts, with no automatic check against existing reservations before a new one was confirmed.

Approach

Automated validation, conflict detection, and calendar sync.

I developed automated booking and operational tracking workflows using Google Apps Script, Google Calendar, Google BigQuery, and Google Sheets, so new bookings are checked against existing ones and synced automatically instead of manually cross-referenced.

Result

Booking conflicts cut by roughly 60%.

The automated workflow reduced booking conflicts by approximately 60%, freeing up time that had been going into manual reconciliation and giving a more reliable view of real-time availability.
