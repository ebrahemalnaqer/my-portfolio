REC-003 — Applied Machine Learning

AI-Driven Solar Panel Tracking Model

Fixed-tilt solar installations lose energy every hour they're not pointed straight at the sun. I built a Python model to predict the optimal dual-axis position in real time and measured how much of that loss it could recover.

Type
Personal / research project
Stack
Python, ML
Accuracy
84% dual-axis alignment
Date
Aug 2025
Problem

Fixed-tilt panels leave energy on the table.

Solar irradiance moves throughout the day, but most low-cost installations use a fixed panel angle. That mismatch between panel position and sun position is a direct, measurable energy loss — the question was how much of it a lightweight tracking model could recover.

Approach

Simulate the sensor inputs, train the model to chase the sun.

I built a Python machine learning model that processes real-time simulated sensor inputs — standing in for the irradiance and positional data a physical dual-axis tracker would read — and outputs the optimal panel angle on both axes, continuously adjusting as conditions change.

Result

84% positioning accuracy, and a meaningful cut in projected losses.

In simulated environments, the model achieved 84% dual-axis positioning alignment accuracy, and projected power losses from misalignment dropped significantly compared to a traditional fixed-tilt baseline — evidence that a relatively lightweight model can recover a meaningful share of the energy fixed installations give up.
