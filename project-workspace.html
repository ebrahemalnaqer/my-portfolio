REC-002 — Analytics Case Study

Workspace Data Analytics & Business Intelligence

Two years of daily operations had produced 900+ Excel files — useful in the moment, unusable as a whole. I consolidated and cleaned them into a single dataset the business could actually analyze.

Role
Data Analyst
Source
900+ daily Excel files, 2 years
Stack
Python · Excel · Power BI
Date
Jul 2026
Problem

900+ scattered files, none of them consistent.

The raw records were fragmented across daily Excel exports, with duplicates, missing timestamps, multilingual customer-name variations, invalid phone numbers, and everyday data-entry errors — none of it usable for analysis as-is.

Approach

Python consolidation, then cleaning and standardization.

I consolidated 900+ daily Excel files collected over two years using Python into a unified dataset, cleaning and standardizing the inconsistent manual data — resolving duplicates, missing timestamps, multilingual customer-name variations, invalid phone numbers, and data-entry errors.

Result

A dataset ready for Power BI, and dashboards to use it.

With the dataset prepared for Power BI, I developed dashboards covering workspace utilization, customer activity, and operational performance — giving the business a recurring view instead of a one-off Excel export.
